ACCOUNTS FOR LOCAL CONDITIONS
Fresh snow, a thaw, rain on snow, a hard refreeze overnight: each leaves the snowpack in a different state and wants a different wax. GetGrip tracks snowfall, rain, thaw-freeze cycles, temperature trends and humidity to tell new snow from fine-grained from a refrozen crust. For glide you choose between new, fine-grained, coarse and wet snow — the distinctions that actually change a glide wax.
