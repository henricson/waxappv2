GetGrip recommends cross-country ski wax from local weather.

To review without a subscription, launch with the argument
  -GGDemoMode YES
which pins a demo location, weather history and full access.

Wax temperature ranges are taken from the manufacturers' own published
charts (swixsport.com and Rode distributor data). The app is not
affiliated with either brand and says so in the description.

In-app purchases: an annual and a monthly subscription (14-day free trial
on annual), and a non-consumable lifetime unlock
(com.squarewave.getgrip.lifetime). The paywall offers all three; Restore
Purchases restores either.

Widgets: add "Wax of the Day" from the Home Screen widget gallery. Long-press
it and choose Edit Widget to switch between current location and a fixed
place, and between grip, glide or both.
